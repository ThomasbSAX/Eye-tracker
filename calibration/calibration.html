Gaze calibration

A dot moves across the screen while the webcam films you. Its position is the exact label of every frame: about 3 minutes give nearly 5,000 labelled frames to code/calibrate.py.

0 · Screen

1 · Camera

Webcam off

2 · Session

  1. Sit 40 to 60 cm away, face well lit (light from the front, no backlight).
  2. Follow the dot with your eyes; you can move your head normally.
  3. Between rounds, change your head position a little as instructed.
  4. Escape stops the session; the video can still be downloaded.

Result

Put both files in data/calibration/, then run code/calibrate.py.
